# Requirements matrix

One row per requirement or test ID: its status, the evidence behind that status, and what the evidence does not prove.

Status is one evidence label (`proposed`, `local-tested`, `abstract-model`, `fork-tested`, `live-read-verified`, `live-testnet-mined`, `historical-replay`, `user-observed`), or `blocked`/`not_started` while the complete required evidence does not exist. Partial evidence is described in the row but never raises its status.

## Evidence sources

- **Live campaign logs.** `evidence/campaign/actions.jsonl` records the Gate claim (#1), Claim A (#2) and Claim B (#3). `evidence/c5/actions.jsonl` records Claim C (#4), the repeat-round claim. Source is Sepolia (EVM 11155111), destination is Creditcoin CC3 testnet (EVM 102031).
- **Claim C.** Round 1 was reserved, never funded, and cancelled after its deadline. Round 2 was reserved, funded into BOUND, assigned after a finalized-funding preflight, and settled with a continuity-refreshed assignment proof; seller and fee credits were withdrawn. The round-1 cancellation proof was refused against round 2 with `SaleIdMismatch` (eth_call). The claim was redeemed to the buyer at maturity. Market liabilities read zero afterwards. The independent `c5-verify` check verifies all 17 recorded stages with none missing; its seven Claim C lines pass in the committed verification report [`evidence/blobs/954be668fd213574fb3790563b6c02d684a79f309bb761029354ea8aa362cbd0.json`](../evidence/blobs/954be668fd213574fb3790563b6c02d684a79f309bb761029354ea8aa362cbd0.json).
- **Local tests.** `node scripts/check-backend.mjs` runs 13 backend commands. The latest run passes 282 tests: 85 contract, 3 protocol, 90 SDK, 81 reference, 23 worker. Contract tests live in `contracts/test/unit/`; unit tests mock native proof verification unless a row says otherwise.
- **Keyless live verification.** `pnpm verify:submission` rechecks deployed runtimes, Claim A economics, proofs, refusals, liabilities and Claim C. See [SUBMISSION_VERIFICATION.md](SUBMISSION_VERIFICATION.md).
- **Limits.** Protocol, campaign and verification limits are collected in [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
